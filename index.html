Privacy Policy

Planet Merge · Effective 26 November 2026

Planet Merge (the "App") is published by Vishudh Tech Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the App does with your information. The short version: we do not collect personal information. Anonymous usage and crash data is collected only if you choose to allow it, and you can turn it off at any time.

Information we collect

By default, none. The App has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Optional usage and crash data (only if you allow it)

The App can ask whether you want to help improve it by sharing anonymous usage and crash data. Nothing is collected unless you tap Allow, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Usage & crash data. If you allow it, the App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect:

  • game events, such as starting or finishing a game, score, the highest planet reached, power-ups used, achievements unlocked, tutorial completion and settings changes;
  • screens viewed;
  • crash reports and diagnostics, such as the app version, Android version, device model and the technical state of the app when it crashed;
  • a randomly generated app-instance identifier that is not linked to your identity.

This data is used only to find bugs and understand which parts of the game people enjoy, so we can improve it. It is processed by Google on our behalf under Firebase's privacy terms. The App does not use the Android advertising ID, does not show ads, and does not use this data for advertising or share it with anyone else. If you decline or turn it off, no usage or crash data is sent.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the App saves the following on your device only:

  • your best scores and game statistics (merges, games played);
  • the planets you have discovered and your achievements;
  • Daily Challenge and daily-reward progress (dates and streak);
  • your settings (sound and vibration).

This data does not leave your device through the App. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings.

Android backup

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may back up the saved data above to your own Google account and restore it on a new device. This is handled by Android and Google under your Google account settings; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android settings.

Permissions

The VIBRATE permission is used for haptic feedback during play. The App also holds network permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), which are used only to send the optional usage and crash data described above, and only if you allow it. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no links that take players out of the App. Optional usage and crash data is anonymous and off until the player allows it; a parent or guardian can leave it off or switch it off in Settings.

Security

Your saved game data stays in Android's app sandbox. Optional usage and crash data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
